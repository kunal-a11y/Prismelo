## What the company does

Prismelo works across the layers a modern business depends on:

- **Application engineering** — custom software, web platforms and APIs.
- **Security** — authorized penetration testing, security architecture and risk advisory.
- **Infrastructure** — cloud planning, deployment, hosting architecture and reliability.
- **Applied AI** — features, integrations and workflow automation with measurable outcomes.

Client work and product work run alongside each other. HirenextAI is the group's active product company.

## How the company is organised

Prismelo is the parent company. Products and subsidiaries keep their own identity, domain and product direction, while drawing on the same engineering and security practice.

No further companies are announced. New entities are added to the [companies page](/companies) only when they exist.
