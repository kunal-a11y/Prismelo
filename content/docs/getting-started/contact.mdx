## Channels

**Website form** — [/contact](/contact) is the preferred route for new enquiries. It asks for the information needed to respond usefully.

**Email** — [hello@prismelo.tech](mailto:hello@prismelo.tech) for general enquiries, existing engagements and documentation corrections.

**Security issues** — do not use the general form. Follow the [responsible disclosure](/docs/services/security-disclosure) process so the report reaches the right place.

## What to include in a first message

A useful first message answers four things:

- **The problem** — what is happening now, and what should be happening instead.
- **The system** — what exists today: stack, hosting, team, constraints.
- **The timeline** — any fixed date, and what it depends on.
- **The decision** — who needs to approve the work.

You do not need a specification. A few clear paragraphs are more useful than a template.

## Response

We aim to reply to enquiries as soon as practical. If a request falls outside what we do, we will say so directly and, where possible, point to the kind of specialist who can help.

## During an engagement

Communication runs through an agreed channel for the duration of the work: a shared project space, email thread or scheduled call. For security assessments, an escalation contact and out-of-hours number are recorded in the rules of engagement before testing starts.

## Confidentiality

Material shared during an enquiry is treated as confidential by default. We can work under your NDA, and can provide a mutual agreement where one is required before technical detail is exchanged.
