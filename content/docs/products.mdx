Prismelo builds client systems and operates its own companies. This section documents the products in the group.

## Current products

| Product | Industry | Status | Relationship |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [HirenextAI](https://hirenextai.com) | AI / Recruitment technology | Active | Owned and operated by Prismelo |

HirenextAI is a company within the Prismelo group. It has its own name, domain and product direction, and product documentation is published by HirenextAI itself.

## Not announced

No further products or subsidiaries are announced. Names, logos and dates are not published ahead of a real launch — so if something is not on this page, it does not exist publicly yet.

## Products in the group versus client work

Client systems remain the client's property. Products in the group are built, owned and operated by Prismelo. The two share engineering standards and security practice, which is deliberate: product work keeps the practice sharp, and client work exposes it to different constraints.

## Product documentation

Each product maintains its own documentation and support route. For HirenextAI, see the [product page](/docs/products/hirenextai) for the relationship and where to find product information.
