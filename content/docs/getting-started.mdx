Prismelo is a technology and cybersecurity company. We work on software, cloud infrastructure, applied AI and security — usually in combination, because systems do not respect those boundaries.

This page explains the working model. The [engagement process](/docs/getting-started/engagement-process) page covers the sequence in detail.

## What an engagement looks like

Most work begins with a conversation about a problem rather than a spec. From that we produce a short written scope: what will be delivered, what is explicitly excluded, what we need from your side, and how success will be judged.

That document matters. It is the reference for every subsequent decision, including the ones that change direction.

<Callout title="Small scope, honest scope" tone="note">
  We would rather define a narrow engagement we can finish well than a broad one that produces partially-finished work. Scope can grow later, deliberately.
</Callout>

## Principles we work to

### Clarity before complexity

The simplest structure that meets the requirement is the one that survives contact with a real team. Complexity is added when it buys something specific — not because it is available.

### Security as a property

Security is a property of how a system is designed, built and operated. It is not a review that happens at the end, and it is not a document.

### Evidence over assertion

Claims about performance, reliability or accuracy should be measurable. Where something is not yet proven — an AI use case, a migration outcome — we say so and propose how to test it.

### Ownership after handover

Work is complete when your team can operate, extend and explain the system without us in the room. That is why documentation is a deliverable, not an extra.

## What we need from you

- A decision-maker who can confirm scope.
- Access to the systems, repositories or environments in scope — or a plan for how access will be provided.
- An honest account of constraints: timeline, budget, existing commitments and team capacity.
- A technical contact who can review work as it is delivered.
