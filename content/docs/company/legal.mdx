## Policies published on this site

- [Privacy Policy](/privacy) — what data this website processes, why, and how to request deletion.
- [Terms of Service](/terms) — the terms governing use of this website.

These pages describe the website. Client engagements are governed by a separate written agreement, which takes precedence for that work.

## Confidentiality

Enquiries, engagements and assessment material are treated as confidential by default. Prismelo can work under a client NDA, and can provide a mutual confidentiality agreement where one is required before technical detail is exchanged.

Confidentiality expectations for security work — including handling of findings and evidence — are recorded in the rules of engagement for each assessment.

## Intellectual property

Code, documentation and configuration produced during an engagement are assigned to the client as set out in the engagement agreement. In the absence of a specific clause, the default position is full transfer on final payment.

Prismelo retains ownership of its brand, documentation templates, internal tooling and any pre-existing components, which may be licensed for use within the delivered system.

## Data handling

This website's contact form processes the information you submit in order to respond to your enquiry. Where delivery email is configured, submissions are transmitted to Prismelo through a transactional email provider.

- No analytics profiles are built from form submissions.
- Submission data is not sold or shared for marketing.
- Retention follows the engagement: enquiry records are kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation and any resulting work.

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) contains the full detail.

## Security reports

Security issues in Prismelo-operated systems should be reported through the [responsible disclosure](/docs/services/security-disclosure) process, not through general support channels.

## Not legal advice

This documentation is operational guidance, not legal advice. Contractual questions should be directed to the contact named in your engagement agreement.
