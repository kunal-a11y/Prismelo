This page is for anyone producing material that carries the Prismelo name — proposals, documentation, product pages or partner collateral.

## Name usage

| Context | Correct | Incorrect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full name | Prismelo.tech | Prismelo Tech, PrismeloTech |
| Wordmark | PRISMELO with .TECH | Prismelo.tech in all caps |
| In running text | Prismelo | PRISMELO |
| Possessive | Prismelo's | Prismelos |

Use **Prismelo.tech** the first time the name appears in a formal document. After that, **Prismelo** is sufficient.

## The symbol

The Prismelo symbol is an abstract loop formed from three rounded segments. It is drawn as a single-colour vector.

- White symbol on black backgrounds.
- Black symbol on white backgrounds when required.
- Do not recolour, outlined-version, skew, add strokes or re-draw the symbol.
- Do not place the symbol on photography or gradients.
- Keep clear space of at least the symbol's width on all sides.

The symbol is used in the navigation, footer, favicon and loading states. It should not be accompanied by a container, badge or icon treatment.

## Colour

The system is strictly monochrome.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | `#000000` | Page background |
| Ink | `#FFFFFF` | Primary text |
| Muted | `#A0A0A0` | Secondary text |
| Line | `#252525` | Borders and rules |
| Surface | `#0A0A0A` | Cards and panels |

No accent colours, gradients or neon treatments are used in brand material. If a diagram needs to show a distinction, use value — line weight, fill lightness, or dashing — rather than hue.

## Typography

Geist is the primary typeface, with Geist Mono used for labels, code and data. When Geist is unavailable, Inter is the permitted substitute.

- Headings are medium weight with tight tracking; they are never bold-heavy.
- Section labels are uppercase, small, with wide tracking.
- Body text is neutral grey on black; primary statements may be white.
- Line length is held to a comfortable measure — long paragraphs are a misuse.

## Voice

Write plainly and specifically. Prefer a concrete statement to a superlative. Avoid claiming leadership, disruption or guaranteed outcomes. If a capability is emerging, describe what exists today.
